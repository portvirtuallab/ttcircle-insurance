THE SIMULATOR

Five modules, one shipment

Each module works on its own, but the data carries forward: the risk score feeds the premium, the premium feeds the policy, and the policy is what a claim is settled against.

01 → STAGE ONE

Risk assessment

 02 → STAGE TWO

 03 → STAGE THREE

Report your payment

 04 → STAGE FOUR

 05 → STAGE FIVE

Insurance claims

ABOUT

Built for the Port Virtual Lab

TTCircle is the insurance operator of the PVL.ONE training environment. This simulator lets participants price and manage cargo cover on the shipments they build in the other PVL modules, using the same Incoterms, documents and vocabulary they meet in the commercial, import, export and airfreight exercises.
