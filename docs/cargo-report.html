Why this matters

What the underwriter looks for.

  • Note the damage on the delivery receipt before the driver leaves — an unqualified receipt is hard to argue against later.
  • Give the carrier written notice of non-apparent damage: 7 days by road (CMR art. 30.1, Sundays and public holidays excluded), 14 days by air (Montreal art. 31.2), 3 days by sea (Hague-Visby art. III r. 6).
  • Loss caused by delay runs on a different clock: 21 days from the day the goods were placed at the consignee's disposal, both by road (CMR art. 30.3) and by air (Montreal art. 31.2).
  • Tell the insurer immediately. Do not repair or dispose of anything before the survey.
  • Photographs of the seal, the container exterior, the stow and the damage itself
  • The transport document and the delivery receipt with the remark
  • Packing list and commercial invoice
  • Survey report, where the loss justifies one
  • Your claim against the carrier and their reply
