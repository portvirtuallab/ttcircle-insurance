    How TTCircle reads the numbers.

    • A · Very low
      Stable markets, mature logistics infrastructure.
    • B · Low
      Favourable conditions, occasional operational friction.
    • C · Medium
      Caution advised — check documents and transit times.
    • D · High
      Enhanced coverage needed; political or security exposure.
      • Regional political stability of origin and destination
